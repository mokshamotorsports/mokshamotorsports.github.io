ABOUT THE TEAM

Moksha Motorsports is the official student BAJA SAE team of IIT Gandhinagar.

We design, manufacture, and race all-terrain vehicles built to tackle the toughest challenges of off-road motorsport. From chassis and suspension to brakes and powertrain, our team combines engineering, hands-on fabrication, and rigorous testing to build reliable, competition-ready machines.

Driven by innovation. Built for the terrain.
