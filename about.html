ABOUT THE TEAM

MOKSHA MOTORSPORTS

WHO WE ARE

Moksha Motorsports is the student BAJA SAE team of IIT Gandhinagar, bringing together students who share a passion for engineering, innovation, and off-road motorsport. We design, manufacture, test, and race our own all-terrain vehicle, turning classroom concepts into machines built to take on challenging terrain.

OUR MISSION

"To engineer capable, reliable, and competitive off-road machines while building the next generation of hands-on engineers."

OUR JOURNEY

2018

THE BEGINNING

Moksha Motorsports was founded in 2018, with the vision of building an IIT Gandhinagar team that could take on the challenge of BAJA SAE.

2024

THE REVIVAL

After a period of inactivity, Moksha Motorsports was revived in 2024 by a new generation of students determined to bring the team back to the track.
