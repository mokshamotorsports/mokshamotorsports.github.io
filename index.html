Who We Are

Ideate, design and build high performance vehicles and compete on the national and international arena.

Moksha Motorsports is the official student‑driven electric racing team of IIT Gandhinagar. We strive to innovate, design, and build high‑performance electric race cars that compete at national and international levels. Our team comprises passionate engineers, designers, and racers who collaborate to push the boundaries of sustainable motorsport technology.
